Account & Data Deletion

ViaHimalaya

We respect your privacy and your right to control your data. If you wish to delete your ViaHimalaya account and all associated data, we will process your request promptly.

📋 What Will Be Deleted

  • Account Information: Email, name, and profile picture
  • Trek History: All recorded trek paths and routes
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates and sensor data
  • Downloaded Content: Offline maps and trek data
  • App Settings: Preferences and configurations

⚠️ Important Notice

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Please ensure you have backed up any trek data you wish to keep before proceeding.

How to Request Deletion

Step 1: Click the button below to open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request.

📧 Send Deletion Request

Step 2: In the email, replace [Enter your email here] with the email address you used to sign in to ViaHimalaya.

Step 3: Send the email. We will process your request and confirm once deletion is complete.

⏱️ Processing Timeline

  • Acknowledgment: Within 2 business days
  • Data Deletion: Completed within 30 days
  • Confirmation: Email sent when deletion is complete

Alternative Method

Data Retention Policy

After deletion, some anonymized data may be retained for:

  • Legal compliance and regulatory requirements
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Aggregated analytics (non-personally identifiable)

This retained data will not contain any personally identifiable information.
