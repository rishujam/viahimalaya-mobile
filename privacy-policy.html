Privacy Policy for ViaHimalaya

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

ViaHimalaya ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you sign in to ViaHimalaya using Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Email address - Used for account identification and authentication, and stored with the trek paths and messages you send us (see 1.2 and 1.6)
  • Name - Displayed in your profile
  • Profile picture - Displayed in your profile
  • User ID - A unique identifier that Firebase Authentication assigns to your account

This information is provided by Google through Firebase Authentication. We use it to sign you in, to display your profile, and to link the data described in this policy to your account.

1.2 Location Data

While the App is open, it uses your device's precise location (through Google Play services) to show where you are on the map. The App does not request background location access, so it does not collect location when you are not using it.

When you tap Start trekking on a trek, the App also records your path and uploads it to our servers. Each recorded point includes:

  • GPS coordinates - Latitude and longitude, with the time each point was recorded
  • Altitude - From GPS and, where your device has one, the barometer
  • Accuracy, speed and bearing - As reported by your device
  • Battery level - Your device's battery percentage at that moment
  • Sensor readings - See 1.3
Important: Recorded paths are stored on our servers together with your email address and the trek you were on. We use them to:
  • Check and improve the accuracy of trek routes, elevation data and navigation
  • Analyze how routes are walked and how the App performs in the field

The App does not currently show recorded paths back to you, and they are not synced between your devices.

1.3 Sensor Data

While you are recording a trek, the App reads your device's motion and environment sensors and attaches the readings to each recorded point (see 1.2):

  • Accelerometer data - For motion detection
  • Gyroscope data - For orientation and movement tracking
  • Barometer data - For altitude and elevation measurements
  • Magnetometer data - For compass direction

This sensor data is uploaded with your recorded path and is used to improve navigation and elevation accuracy.

1.4 Trek Activity Data

We collect information about your trek activity:

  • Which trek you recorded - The trek you were on when you started a recording, and the start time and duration of that recording
  • Downloaded treks - Trek maps and data you download for offline use are stored on your device. Our servers see the download request like any other internet request (see 1.5), but we do not link it to your account.

1.5 Device, Usage and Crash Information

We use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to find and fix crashes. These services collect:

  • Usage events - For example, that the App was opened, and when a sign-in error occurs
  • Crash reports - When the App crashes: the technical details of the crash and the state of the App at that moment
  • Device information - Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, App version, language, and your approximate region (derived from your IP address)
  • Identifiers - A random installation ID generated by Firebase, an app-specific user ID that is not your email address, and your device's advertising ID if one is available

Our servers (see Section 9) also receive the technical information that accompanies any internet request, such as your IP address, the time of the request and the type of device.

1.6 Messages You Send Us

If you use the in-app form to request a trek or send feedback, we store your message together with your email address and the time you sent it. We use it to read and respond to your request and to decide which treks to add.

1.7 Places and Photos You Add

The App lets you add a place to a trek, such as a campsite or a source of water, with a photo, an optional short note (up to 300 characters) and a position. When you tap Save, we store:

  • The photo - Shrunk on your device before it is sent. The location and camera details that phones record inside photos are removed from the copy we keep
  • The note - If you wrote one
  • The position - Either your phone's location when you saved, with the GPS accuracy if your phone reported one, or the spot you chose on the map; we record which of the two it was
  • Details of the submission - The trek it was added to, the time, your account's user ID, and the version of our Content Terms you agreed to

If you save with no internet connection, the place waits on your device and is sent automatically when the App next has a connection.

Photos are kept in private storage. Nobody else can see a place you add until we have looked at it and approved it, and we may reject any place. Once a place is approved, other users of the App may see its photo, note and position. We do not show your name or email address with it.

2.3 Support

  • Requests and Feedback - To read and respond to the trek requests and feedback you send us

2.4 Places You Add

  • Review and Display - To check the places and photos you add and, once approved, to show them to other users of the App
  • Trek Information - To improve our trek data, for example the location of campsites and water

The photo, note and position of a place you add can be shown to other users of the App once we have approved it. They are not shown with your name or email address. You can ask us to remove a place at any time (see Section 5.2). By adding a place you agree to our Content Terms.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Your Data is Stored

  • Local Storage: Trek maps, downloaded routes, your recorded trek paths, and cached data are stored locally on your device
  • Cloud Storage: Your sign-in account is held by Firebase Authentication (Google). A copy of your recorded trek paths, the messages you send us and the details of places you add are stored in our database, which is hosted by Neon; our servers run on Cloudflare, which also holds the photos of places you add in private storage

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data stored on our servers is encrypted
  • Sign-in: Handled by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication - we never see or store a password
  • Access Controls: Limited access to user data by authorized personnel only
  • Regular Security Audits: Periodic review of security practices
Please note: No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

5.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account Information: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Recorded Trek Paths and Sensor Data: Retained on our servers, linked to your email address, until you ask us to delete them
  • Messages You Send Us: Retained, linked to your email address, until you ask us to delete them
  • Places and Photos You Add: Retained, linked to your account's user ID, until you ask us to delete them or we remove them
  • Analytics and Crash Data: Held by Google and kept for the periods set in Google's own policies
  • Cached Data: Local device data is retained until you clear app data or uninstall

5.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us at rishuparashar7@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.

Upon your request, we will:

  • Delete your account information within 30 days
  • Remove your recorded trek paths, sensor data and messages from our database
  • Remove the places and photos you added from our database and storage
  • Delete the analytics data associated with your user ID where Google's tools allow it

Some data may be retained for legal or security purposes as required by law.
