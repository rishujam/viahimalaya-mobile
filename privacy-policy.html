Privacy Policy for ViaHimalaya

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

ViaHimalaya ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you sign in to ViaHimalaya using Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Email address - Used for account identification and authentication
  • Name - Displayed in your profile
  • Profile picture - Displayed in your profile

This information is provided by Google through Firebase Authentication and is used solely for user authentication and profile display purposes.

1.2 Location Data

We collect precise location data when you use the App for trek navigation:

  • GPS coordinates - Real-time latitude, longitude, and altitude
  • Location accuracy - GPS accuracy measurements
  • Location timestamps - When each location point was recorded
  • Trek paths - The complete route you follow during a trek
Important: Your location data is collected during active trek sessions and is synced to our servers to:
  • Enable trek history and statistics
  • Provide route analysis and insights
  • Improve trek route recommendations
  • Allow you to access your trek history across devices

1.3 Sensor Data

We collect data from your device sensors during trek tracking:

  • Accelerometer data - For motion detection and step counting
  • Gyroscope data - For orientation and movement tracking
  • Barometer data - For altitude and elevation measurements
  • Magnetometer data - For compass direction

This sensor data is used to enhance navigation accuracy and provide detailed trek statistics.

1.4 Trek Activity Data

We collect information about your trek activities:

  • Trek routes taken - Which treks you have completed
  • Trek statistics - Distance traveled, elevation gain, duration
  • Trek timestamps - Start and end times of your treks
  • Downloaded treks - Which trek maps you have downloaded for offline use

1.5 Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers (for crash reporting and analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Functionality

  • Authentication - To verify your identity and provide secure access
  • Navigation - To show your real-time position on trek routes
  • Trek Tracking - To record your trek path and generate statistics
  • Offline Maps - To provide downloaded trek maps for offline use
  • Profile Management - To display your account information

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Route Analysis - To analyze popular routes and improve trek recommendations
  • App Performance - To identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Feature Development - To understand which features are most used
  • Quality Assurance - To ensure accurate GPS tracking and navigation

2.3 User Experience

  • Trek History - To show your completed treks and statistics
  • Cross-Device Sync - To access your trek data on multiple devices
  • Personalization - To recommend treks based on your activity

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Your Data is Stored

  • Local Storage: Trek maps, downloaded routes, and cached data are stored locally on your device
  • Cloud Storage: Your account information, trek history, and synced trek paths are stored securely on our servers (Firebase/Google Cloud)

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data stored on our servers is encrypted
  • Authentication: Secure authentication via Google Sign-In and Firebase
  • Access Controls: Limited access to user data by authorized personnel only
  • Regular Security Audits: Periodic review of security practices
Please note: No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

5.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account Information: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Trek History: Retained indefinitely unless you request deletion
  • Location Data: Trek paths are retained to provide historical records
  • Sensor Data: Aggregated sensor data may be retained for analytics
  • Cached Data: Local device data is retained until you clear app data or uninstall

5.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by:

  • Deleting your account through the App settings or contacting us at rishuparashar7@gmail.com

Upon account deletion, we will:

  • Delete your account information within 30 days
  • Remove your trek history and location data
  • Anonymize any aggregated analytics data

Some data may be retained for legal or security purposes as required by law.
